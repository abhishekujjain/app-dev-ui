Local Weather Forecast

Also known as Mausam — a free Android weather app for checking current conditions and planning ahead, whether you are at home, travelling, or checking if rain is on the way.

Get Local Weather Forecast on Google Play

Weather details at a glance

Check weather for your current location or search for a city or place. Local Weather Forecast keeps the information you need clear and easy to scan.

What you can do with Local Weather Forecast (Mausam)
  • GPS camera - Add map, location & weather stamps to photos and videos
  • Local Map GPS Direction - Find nearby places, check coordinates, and explore local places
  • Digital Compass - Simple, accurate compass for everyday direction
  • GPS Coordinates - Find, save, and share GPS latitude and longitude
  • ABC Kids Learn - ABC phonics and letter tracing for kids
  • Ludo Champs Game - Offline Ludo for friends and family
