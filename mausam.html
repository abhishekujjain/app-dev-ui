Mausam: Weather & Forecast

Mausam is a simple Android weather app for checking current conditions and planning ahead, whether you are at home, travelling, or checking if rain is on the way.

Get Mausam on Google Play

Weather details at a glance

Check weather for your current location or search for a city or place. Mausam keeps the information you need clear and easy to scan.

What you can do with Mausam
  • See current and feels-like temperature, conditions and wind speed.
  • Check humidity and air quality index (AQI).
  • View hourly weather updates and a seven-day forecast.
  • Choose Celsius or Fahrenheit, your preferred theme and language.

About Mausam

Mausam is published by Intelligent Mind. Location is used only when you allow it to provide weather for your current area; you can search for any location instead.
