Weather

Also known as Mausam — a free Android weather app for checking current conditions and planning ahead, whether you are at home, travelling, or checking if rain is on the way.

Get Weather on Google Play

Weather details at a glance

Check weather for your current location or search for a city or place. Weather keeps the information you need clear and easy to scan.

What you can do with Weather (Mausam)
  • See current and feels-like temperature, conditions and wind speed.
  • Check humidity and air quality index (AQI).
  • View hourly weather updates and a seven-day forecast.
  • Choose Celsius or Fahrenheit, your preferred theme and language.

About Weather

Weather is published by Intelligent Mind on Google Play (package online.intelligentmind.mausam). Location is used only when you allow it to provide weather for your current area; you can search for any location instead.
