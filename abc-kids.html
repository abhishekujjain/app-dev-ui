ABC Kids

ABC Kids is the free Android app from Intelligent mind, listed on Google Play as ABC Kids Learn. Help toddlers learn the alphabet with phonics, letter tracing, and fun rewards.

Get ABC Kids on Google Play

Alphabet learning for toddlers

Big, easy-to-tap letter cards with phonics sounds, guided tracing, and personalized learning — made for preschoolers and kindergarteners.

ABC Kids highlights
  • GPS camera - Add map, location & weather stamps to photos and videos
  • Local Map GPS Direction - Find nearby places, check coordinates, and explore local places
  • Digital Compass - Simple, accurate compass for everyday direction
  • GPS Coordinates - Find, save, and share GPS latitude and longitude
  • Local Weather Forecast - Accurate weather, hourly updates, 7-day forecasts, and AQI
  • Ludo Champs Game - Offline Ludo for friends and family
