Lat long: GPS Coordinates

Lat long is a free Android app for finding, saving and sharing precise latitude, longitude and altitude.

Get Lat long on Google Play

Find and share precise coordinates

Use your current GPS location or tap a point on the map when you need the coordinates for a place.

What you can do with Lat long
  • See live latitude, longitude and altitude.
  • Use decimal degrees or degrees, minutes and seconds (DMS).
  • Tap anywhere on a map to identify coordinates.
  • Save and share exact locations with ease.

About Lat long

Lat long is published by Intelligent Mind. It uses location permission only to provide your current position; you can manage this in Android settings at any time.
