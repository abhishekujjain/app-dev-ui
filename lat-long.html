GPS Coordinates

Also known as Lat long or latitude longitude on Google Play — a free Android app for finding, saving and sharing precise latitude, longitude and altitude.

Get GPS Coordinates on Google Play

Find and share precise coordinates

Use your current GPS location or tap a point on the map when you need the coordinates for a place. GPS Coordinates keeps everything clear and easy to copy or share.

What you can do with GPS Coordinates (Lat long)
  • GPS camera - Add map, location & weather stamps to photos and videos
  • Local Map GPS Direction - Find nearby places, check coordinates, and explore local places
  • Digital Compass - Simple, accurate compass for everyday direction
  • Local Weather Forecast - Accurate weather, hourly updates, 7-day forecasts, and AQI
  • ABC Kids Learn - ABC phonics and letter tracing for kids
  • Ludo Champs Game - Offline Ludo for friends and family
