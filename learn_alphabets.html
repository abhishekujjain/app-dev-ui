Frequently Asked Questions

What age is this alphabet tool for?

Preschoolers and young children (ages 3–7) learning their ABCs, letter sounds and early phonics.

How does the Phonics button work?

Tap Phonics on any letter card to hear the letter name, sound and example word spoken aloud — for example, "A is for Apple. Ah, ah, apple!"
