App FAQs

Find the Right Android App

Quick answers about the Intelligent mind apps available on Google Play.

Which apps are available?

GPS camera stamps photos with GPS data; Local Map GPS Direction helps you discover nearby places; Digital Compass helps with orientation; GPS Coordinates lets you find and share coordinates; Local Weather Forecast provides forecasts and AQI; ABC Kids Learn teaches the alphabet; and Ludo Champs Game brings classic offline Ludo to your phone.

What does Local Weather Forecast do?

Local Weather Forecast (Mausam) shows current conditions, feels-like temperature, wind, humidity, AQI, hourly updates and a seven-day forecast for your location or a place you search.

How do I install an app?

Select an app’s Google Play button. On Android, the link opens the Play Store directly when it is available on your device.

What does GPS camera do?

Stamp photos and videos with GPS coordinates, map, address, date, time and weather — perfect for site documentation, travel and field work.

What is the GPS camera app?

GPS camera is listed on Google Play — stamp photos and videos with location, map, coordinates, date, time and weather. Also known as Geo Camera Map.

What is the compass app?

compass is the same free app as Digital Compass — check your heading for hiking, travel and everyday navigation on Android.

What is latitude longitude?

latitude longitude is the same app as GPS Coordinates (Lat long) — find, save and share precise GPS coordinates.

What is the localmap app?

localmap is the same free app as Local Map GPS Direction — find nearby restaurants, cafés, hotels, shops and get GPS directions on Android.

What can I do with GPS Coordinates?

Find, save, and share precise latitude and longitude coordinates from your current GPS location or a point selected on a map. Also known as Lat long or latitude longitude on Google Play.

What is ABC Kids Learn?

ABC Kids Learn is a free alphabet learning app for toddlers with phonics, letter tracing, and personalized rewards — perfect for preschoolers and kindergarteners.

What is Ludo Champs Game?

Ludo Champs Game is a free offline Ludo board game for 2–4 players on one Android device. Pass the phone, roll the dice, and race your tokens home — no internet required.
