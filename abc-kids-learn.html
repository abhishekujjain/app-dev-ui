Learn A to Z through play

Browse all 26 letters in a simple, child-friendly layout. Each letter comes with a fun word, emoji, and clear phonics sound.

What you can do with ABC Kids Learn
  • Learn all 26 letters with words, emoji, and phonics sounds.
  • Tap Phonics to hear how each letter sounds.
  • Practice writing letters with guided finger tracing.
  • Personalize learning with your child's name.
  • Earn stars and cheerful animations as letters are completed.
