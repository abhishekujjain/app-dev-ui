Ludo Champs Game

Bring the classic Ludo board to your phone and play anytime, anywhere — no internet, no account, and no waiting for opponents online.

Get Ludo Champs Game on Google Play

Classic Ludo, offline on one device

Choose 2, 3, or 4 players, pick any colour as Human or Computer, and pass the device around for local multiplayer with friends and family.

What you can do with Ludo Champs Game
  • Play 2, 3, or 4 players — Human or Computer opponents.
  • Pass the phone for local multiplayer with friends and family.
  • Roll the dice, move tokens, and send opponents back.
  • Roll a 6 to bring a token out or get an extra turn.
  • Works fully offline — no internet connection needed.
